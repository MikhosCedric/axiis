# Landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Public page for signed-out visitors; signed-in users are sent to /today. Audience: a busy person splitting work and home across a to-do app, a calendar and notes. Job: understand what Axiis is in seconds, believe it by seeing a day drawn as the Score, sign in. Proof is a labelled sample day only: no testimonials, counts, logos, pricing or unshipped features (notes, week view, recurrence only as "coming").

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a printed programme for one day. The Score itself, set full width on the paper, is the hero and the proof. It refuses the split hero with a screenshot and the row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The app's tokens unchanged. Warm music-paper ground, deep ink dark theme, engraved hairlines, staves with hour barlines, item state as line form (solid live, dashed planned, hollow done). Verdigris only for the playhead at now and the one Sign in action. Besley headings, Hanken Grotesk UI, tabular times.

STORY: The visitor reads one plain line on what Axiis is, sees a sample day across Work, Home and Health with a playhead at now, reads the line-form key, follows three movements (capture, give it a time, see how it connects), and signs in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Thin top bar, mark and wordmark left, Sign in right. Left-aligned Besley headline at display scale, one sentence under it, Sign in (verdigris) and a quiet "How it works" link. Below, edge to edge in the content column, the sample Score with axis, three staves, beams in all three states, the playhead, and a caption labelling it a sample day with the key.

FORM: Chamber-music score, index 4 of 7 from the Today prototype, seed 55c92e4f, extended to a Persuade surface. Signature: the playhead sweeps once from the morning to now on load, eased out, from an already-visible score; reduced motion shows it in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
